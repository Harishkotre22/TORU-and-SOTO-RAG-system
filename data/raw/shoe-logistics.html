Smart logistics with the commissioning robot TORU

Auto­mate the stor­age and retrieval of shoe boxes or cus­tom KLTs from shelv­ing racks.

  • Inte­gra­tion
  • Transfer systems
  • Technology & Safety
  • Fleet Management
  • TORU
Inte­gra­tion

Com­mis­sion­ing robot TORU inte­grates eas­ily and with­out major adjust­ments into an exist­ing ware­house and processes. Once con­fig­ured, it works autonomously along­side its human col­leagues. The con­nec­tion to a ware­house man­age­ment sys­tem is uncom­pli­cated via WLAN.

Oper­a­tion

Shoe boxes are its spe­cialty: the intel­li­gent pick­ing robot TORU picks indi­vid­ual boxes — even from stacks — and takes them directly to the trans­fer sta­tion. Of course, it also brings them back again for stor­age or returns. Up to 20 hours a day. This allows him to be active out­side reg­u­lar work shifts and, for exam­ple, smooth out peaks through prepara­tory work. After an update, TORU also picks up spe­cial small load car­ri­ers (KLTs) as an inte­gral part of the NEOin­tral­o­gis­tics logis­tics sys­tem in its proven reli­able manner.

Scal­ing

Pick­ing with TORU not only adapts to your ware­house envi­ron­ment, but also to your needs: New robots can be inte­grated directly, as they inherit their knowl­edge from the TORU(s) already imple­mented. Your robot fleet grows with your success.

Pick­ing robot TORU in action

The mobile pick­ing robot TORU is ideal for sort­ing orders into ded­i­cated com­part­ments and pick­ing up boxes for the stor­age process. It picks indi­vid­ual shoe­boxes or spe­cial NEO-KLTs and trans­ports them to a trans­fer point. Here, TORU can con­sol­i­date dif­fer­ent items in a sin­gle order so that the pack­ag­ing process can be ini­ti­ated directly.

Flex­i­bil­ity for your processes: Trans­fer sta­tions at a glance

 

TORU inte­grates per­fectly into your cur­rent processes. No mat­ter which trans­fer sta­tions you use in your ware­house or pick­ing process. TORU reli­ably han­dles the great­est pos­si­ble vari­ance within an oper­at­ing range of 8 cm to 250 cm above the floor:

Con­veyor belt

Picked car­tons can be placed directly on con­veyor sticks to be fed to a sorter, for exam­ple, or to the auto­matic pack­ing line. 

Flow rack

TORU places car­tons directly into flow racks and picks from them. The han­dling is suit­able for down­stream tote processes as well as sin­gle & multi orders.

Order pick­ing trolley

Shoe boxes can be picked up from or deliv­ered to pick­ing trol­leys. Ideal for sort­ing orders and other inbound & out­bound processes. 

Put­wall

Pick­ing via the put­wall syn­chro­nises pick-by-light com­part­ment dis­plays and thus offers max­i­mum flex­i­bil­ity in sort­ing orders and sub­se­quent processes.

Tech­nol­ogy & safety: Com­mis­sion­ing robot TORU in detail

 

The pick­ing robot TORU helps your human col­leagues to han­dle order pick­ing with sig­nif­i­cantly less effort. It works just about 24/7 and gives you greater inde­pen­dence from order peaks and staff plan­ning dur­ing off-peak shifts, vaca­tion peri­ods or sick­ness waves.

TORU com­bines high-end tech­nol­ogy with intel­li­gent soft­ware and the high­est safety stan­dards for safe work­ing side by side with people.

Fleet Man­age­ment for your TORU Fleet

With ARC (Advanced Robot Con­trol), users always have an overview of the entire fleet, the indi­vid­ual TORU-robot as well as the order man­age­ment and rel­e­vant KPIs:

Con­trol of the robot fleet

ARC is the con­trol cen­ter of the TORU fleet and is con­nected to the inven­tory man­age­ment sys­tem. On a day-to-day basis, ARC not only offers full trans­parency of all robots, order manage­ment and KPIs, but also pro­vides instruc­tions on how to solve problems.

Visual Rep­re­sen­ta­tion

The visual map dis­play in your own ware­house makes con­trol­ling the fleet par­tic­u­larly intu­itive. It shows where the indi­vid­ual robots are on the move in the ware­house, which orders they are pro­cessing, which items they are cur­r­ent­ly trans­port­ing and which actions are com­ing up next.

Track­ing of KPIs

Rel­e­vant KPIs are recorded in real time and clearly dis­played. The data overview offers full trans­parency at all times. For exam­ple, fleet per­for­mance over a period of time, aver­age uti­liza­tion or the cur­rent sta­tus of the trans­fer stations.

Order Man­age­ment

Order man­age­ment ensures opti­mal struc­tur­ing of trans­port orders to guar­an­tee a fast process. Cur­rent orders are dis­trib­uted in the fleet and can be read at any time.

Human Process Coordination

ARC also coor­di­nates the processes between robots and humans. Employ­ees can see directly which pick­ing trol­ley is ready for pick-up.

Remote Oper­a­tions

The remote oper­a­tions team mon­i­tors robot activ­i­ties 24 hours a day, 6 days a week, and can proac­tively assist remotely if needed, increas­ing fleet availability.

Autonomous stor­age and retrieval of shoe boxes or spe­cial KLTs in shelf­ing racks.

Piece-pre­cise order picking

Tar­geted grip­ping and stack­ing of boxes

TORU has a pre­cise vac­uum grip­per arm. This allows it to pick up, lift and place car­tons or spe­cial totes weigh­ing up to 5.8 kg, with a max. size of 390 x 280 x 145 mm (L x W x H), with piece-by-piece pre­ci­sion. Even entire stacks if necessary.

Autonomous per­cep­tion

2D and 3D cameras

TORU locates objects to be picked and free stor­age loca­tions on the shelf. Using intel­li­gent algo­rithms, TORU searches for the bar­codes and can thus iden­tify the cor­rect object.

High trans­port capacity

16 boxes and a wide range of action

TORU can trans­port up to 16 objects simul­ta­ne­ously in its back­pack rack. With its action range of 8 cm to 250 cm above the ground, it even sur­passes human agility — with­out any back pain.

Safety is always the top priority for TORU

Our robots are safe when dealing with people. To this end, we have equipped them with a state-of-the-art person recognition system from certified manufacturers. We also work closely with the occupational safety specialists in our customers’ factories on site to ensure smooth integration.
